# CP Mountain Section — Revelstoke → Rogers Pass → Golden → Field

Prototype ~206 km (km 606-812). Model length at 1:1000 run-compression ~206 m (676 ft).

## Design basis (sliding scale)

- **Equipment:** HO 1:87. 12 ft train = ~1,000-1,200 proto ft (10 well-cars + 2 AC4400 + caboose). Fits any real CP siding (5,000-11,000 ft).
- **Runs:** 1 proto km = 1 model m (1:1000 compression on single track).
- **Sidings:** compressed to **12 ft usable** (4 x 4-ft modules) + ~1 ft turnouts each end + short single-track tails ≈ **16 ft / 4 modules** per siding.
- **Yards:** modelled long enough for the 12 ft train (shortest yard track ≥ 12 ft); full prototype track count not required.
- **Tunnels:** compressed; MacDonald 14.6 km becomes a short scenic portal/mountain module.
- **Curves:** all prototype curves are gentle in HO (true-scale radius ≥ 24 in); you may run tighter for space.
