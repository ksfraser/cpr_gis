# CP Vancouver → Calgary — Multi-Level NOLIX Room Plan

The full **1,029 km** route as one continuous mainline that climbs around a **10 × 10 ft bedroom**: one level per lap, hidden staging below, hidden return above, and a closed loop for continuous running.

Room: **10 × 10 ft** · layout rail **40–64 in** above the floor · storage below **4 ft** (buckets, boxes).

## 1. How it fits the room (NOLIX lap math)

Bench depth **24 in** on the walls; usable mainline per lap ≈ **352 in (29 ft)** (384 in of wall run minus 32 in door allowance).

Level spacing **8 in** rail-to-rail (clearance ≥ 4 in for double-stack wells, hi-cube boxes, double-deck passenger cars).

**Lap grade ≈ 2.27%** — comfortably under the 4% ceiling. 3 visible laps × 8 in = **16 in** of climb from L0 to the top lap; add staging's 8 in and the full transit rises **24 in**.

| lap | subdivisions | rail height | front band |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | Cascade + Thompson | 48 in (main) | Vancouver / Fraser Canyon / Kamloops |
| L1 | Shuswap + Mountain | 56 in (main) | Revelstoke / Rogers Pass / Field |
| L2 | Laggan | 64 in (main) | Golden / Banff / Calgary (top yard) |
| S  | staging | 40 in (hidden) | Vancouver staging, 8 tracks |

Grade is 8 in per ≈30 ft lap — a train does a full Vancouver → Calgary run in three visible laps plus the hidden return. Optional **extra laps** (extra wall passes, deeper lap) give the hidden descent tracks climb room so they also stay under 4%.

## 2. Linear full-route allocation (per subdivision)

Each lap's **front band** carries that subdivision's yard/sidings/industries (12-ft train standard, 6-module yard body); the **back band** carries the mainline at +8 in.  Module counts below are 4-ft bays for the core (CORE) build.

| subdivision | proto km | yards | sidings | tunnels | main run | core mods |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade | 0–209 | 9 | 1 | 4 | 7 | 15 |
| Thompson | 209–403 | 4 | 9 | 4 | 7 | 15 |
| Shuswap | 403–609 | 6 | 5 | 2 | 7 | 15 |
| Mountain | 609–810 | 12 | 24 | 5 | 7 | 15 |
| Laggan | 810–1029 | 5 | 0 | 3 | 7 | 15 |
|  | **1,029 km** |  |  |  |  | **75 ≈ 300 ft** |

Not every prototype yard is built — one representative yard per lap (the largest / most iconic) gets the full 6-module body; the rest appear as front-band tracks, sidings, or are dropped. This is the same representative-compression used on the mountain section.

## 3. Level plan (bottom → top)

| level | rail | hidden? | front band (visible) | back band (main) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 40 in | **hidden** | 8 staging tracks | return descent from L2 |
| L0 | 48 in | no | Vancouver / Fraser Canyon / Kamloops | Cascade + Thompson main, +8 in |
| L1 | 56 in | no | Revelstoke / Rogers Pass / Field | Shuswap + Mountain main, +8 in |
| L2 | 64 in | no | Golden / Banff / Calgary (top yard) | Laggan main, +8 in |

Within one lap the **front band is 0 in** and the main is **+8 in** on the same module — the visible/raised split.  The main climbs the next 8 in as it rounds the corner into the next lap's module row.

Total rise staging → top: **24 in**. The top level (64 in) is reachable from a step stool; keep scenery low-relief there.  If reach is a concern, cap the climb at 2 visible laps and fold the Laggan lap into L1.

## 4. Module cross-section & the nesting box

The module stays a **2-ft-deep stepped wedge** so any two modules still nest inverted into the storage box (2 × 2 × 4 ft pairs). Front-to-back:

```
      front (aisle)                  back (wall)
    0-8 in:  L0 yard/siding track(s)  at 0 in    [visible]
    8-16 in: 45 deg riser             (scenery)
   16-24 in: L1 main track            at +8 in   [visible]
             scenery slope rises 45 deg to the module back
```

Hidden tracks tuck under the riser at the rear of the front band (0 in, behind scenery) — the “back-of-module at 0, hidden” track.  The two 45 deg faces (riser + back slope) let an inverted mate nest in: treads meet treads, slopes interlock, box = 2 × 2 × 4 ft.

Design intent is verified with a cardboard cross-section mock-up before framing; riser steepness (45–60 deg) is a free tuning parameter to guarantee clean nesting.

## 5. Operations — double main as single + passing

The physical run is **double main**; it is *operated* as single track with passing sidings using crossovers:

- **Continuous running (circles):** run one direction on each main — directional.  The hidden return closes the loop.
- **Prototype meets:** line the crossovers so a train changes tracks at each block; the second main *virtually does not exist* through that section — the segment reads as single track with the mate acting as a passing siding.
- Crossovers are **#6** (matching the 89-ft well-car minimum) and are thrown by the local switch list, so the “missing” main can be re-added later for full double-track ops.

This gives the familiar two-train-in-one-scene meets without duplicating the whole subdivision's trackage.

## 6. Storage in the bedroom

- **Below 4 ft:** buckets, boxes, workbench — plain storage, no layout.
- **Layout:** rail heights 40–64 in on wall brackets; nothing on the floor.
- **Modules** come down in 4-ft bays and nest in 2 × 2 × 4 ft boxes (two modules per box).  A lap's yard modules pair with its main modules; staging modules pair among themselves.

## 7. Clearances

- Level-to-level **8 in** rail spacing ≫ 4 in minimum — double-stack well cars, hi-cube boxes, and double-deck passenger cars all clear.
- Main-vs-front-band within a module: 8 in, same headroom.
- Hidden return is 8 in below its visible main — unseen, maintenance access from the aisle.

## 8. Caveats & open questions

- 10 × 10 ft at HO is *signature* scale, not a true scale model: the run is ≈ 1:8,600 rather than 1:1,000.  Each lap carries the *feel* of its subdivision, not every mile.
- Door allowance: the door wall limits the lap to ≈ 28-30 ft; a removable bridge or duckunder section may be needed on the door wall at the lower levels.
- Top-level reach at 64 in needs a step stool; consider capping the climb at 2 visible laps if reach is a concern.
- NOLIX running = long, slow reveals; meets happen where laps overlap visually.  True point-to-point (staging-to-staging with no return) is the discard alternative — it breaks continuous running.
- The room-grade ≈ 2.3% is a *continuous running grade*, not a scene-grade: scenes keep the mountain-section ±2.5% rule on top of it only where the prototype climbs (see `cpr_module_plan-comp.md`).

## 9. Design discussion log (decisions, ideas, discards)

- **Asked:** Bedroom size assumed 10 × 10 ft — user confirmed, with grade ceiling < 4%.
- **Asked:** Level spacing — user accepted 8 in instead of the 12 in example; 8 in rail-to-rail gives ~4 in clear for double-stack.
- **Asked:** Linear scope — user chose the FULL 1,029 km Van→Cal, not just the mountain section.
- **Asked:** Levels — hidden staging (8 tracks) below L0/L1/L2; top level may carry a yard.
- **Asked:** Mainline — double main operated as single + passing via crossovers; directional running for circles.
- **Asked:** Module tracks — user wants multiple tracks at different heights in one module: front 0 in (visible), back 0 in (hidden behind scenery), center ~1 ft (raised), back ~2 ft (raised); three visible track bands plus hidden staging.
- **Idea:** Fold the 1,029 km route into one level per lap — Cascade+Thompson / Shuswap+Mountain / Laggan → three visible laps, each 8 in higher.
- **Idea:** Hidden return on the same bench (8 in below the visible main) so staging connects top to bottom → closed loop for continuous running.
- **Idea:** Module cross-section = front yard band (0 in) + raised main band (+8 in) with a 45 deg riser — keeps the two-triangle nesting box intact.
- **Idea:** Hidden tracks under the riser at the rear of the front band gives the requested “back-of-module at 0, hidden” track.
- **Idea:** Optional extra laps so hidden descent tracks keep a gentle grade (the ~4 in clearance also accommodates dbl-stack/ hi-cube/dbl-deck equipment).
- **Discard:** True 1:1000 linear build of the whole route (1,029 m / ≈ 845 modules) — physically impossible in a bedroom; kept as the linear *reference* only.
- **Discard:** All 34 yards with full 6-module bodies — one representative yard per lap instead.
- **Discard:** Pure mushroom (stacked flat shelves, no climb) — no grade interest, and the main loses the continuous-climb theatre of the NOLIX.
- **Discard:** Staging-to-staging point-to-point with no return — breaks the “run trains in circles” goal.
- **Discard:** A single 24-in module with 4 full track levels — the profile stops being a nesting triangle; the raised bands are instead kept to two levels per module (front 0 + main +8) and the *lap* provides the next 8-in step.
- **Open:** Nesting geometry to be proven with a cardboard mock-up; riser angle is the tuning knob.
- **Open:** Door-wall handling at lower levels (removable bridge vs duckunder vs skipping a short section).
- **Open:** Where the hidden return crosses the door wall.
- **Open:** Mushroom vs NOLIX choice is deferred: the NOLIX is the current working plan; a wall-hugging mushroom variant (flat shelves, no climb) is the fallback if the spiral grades prove unworkable.
