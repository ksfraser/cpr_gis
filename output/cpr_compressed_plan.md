# CP Mountain Section — Compressed Module Plan

Selective compression of the `-proto` 1:1000 designs, built on the unified end-plate standard (`cpr_modular_standard.md`): yards shrink to 5-7 modules so the shortest yard track is the **12 ft** design standard; plain single-track runs shrink to 1-2 omittable scenic modules; tunnels are one portal module each.

| scene | proto km | proto mods | core mods | +optional | build ft |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Revelstoke yard | 18 | 15 | 6 | 6+5 | 24-44 |
| B | Eagle Pass (Monashee) | 33 | 27 | 1 | 1+0 | 4-4 |
| C | Rogers Pass summit | 48 | 40 | 13 | 13+2 | 52-60 |
| D | Beavermouth / Albert Canyon | 23 | 19 | 1 | 1+0 | 4-4 |
| E | Golden | 34 | 28 | 7 | 7+1 | 28-32 |
| F | Yoho Valley to Field | 51 | 42 | 7 | 7+2 | 28-36 |
|  | **total** | **206** | **171** | **35** | **35+10** | **140-180** |

**Reduction:** 171 → 35 core modules (80% fewer); 140-180 ft of bench vs 684 ft at full 1:1000 build.

## Yard bodies (all scenes)

Every modelled yard uses the same 6-module body so the shortest yard track is 12 ft usable:

| bay | module | carries |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | west lead / headshunt | runaround, ladder entry |
| 2 | ladder | #6 turnouts (89-ft well-cars) |
| 3-5 | body | yard tracks, **12 ft usable** |
| 6 | east tail | clearance clear of the main |

See `cpr_yard_plans-proto.md` for the turnout plans; the compressed yards use the identical ladder but on 6 modules instead of the 1:1000 fan.
